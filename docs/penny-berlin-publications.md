# PENNY Berlin regional price publications

PENNY's public market profile binds Emser Str. 1–2, 12051 Berlin (native market `8534449`) to selling region `15A-01-56`. The current native category week and that exact region select its “dauerhaft im Preis gesenkt” category. This provides regional price cards; it does not provide a complete assortment, verified regular shelf prices, GTINs or retailer SKUs.

The collector makes three sequential, bounded public GETs: market profile, category HTML, regional category JSON. It preserves each complete UTF-8 response, original HTTP/capture metadata and SHA-256 hashes in `penny_berlin_publication_captures`. Exact sale packs and cent-rounded base prices must agree. Conflicting same-title/same-pack prices, duplicate document IDs, unsupported conditions and unreviewed identities/validity stay quarantined with their original row index. The complete newest snapshot is selected before searching; a removed, invalid, changed or expired card cannot revive an older price. Different proofs at the same newest capture time withhold that snapshot.

`GET /v1/berlin-reference-prices?search=Schoko&merchant=PENNY&pack=125%20g` returns these in `publications`, alongside the unchanged `items` contract for identified retailer offers. `referenceRows` counts only those ordinary offers; `publicationReferences` counts CMS cards separately. These cards never create canonical products, selected GTINs, checkout totals, physical basket winners or assertions of full coverage. Their source market, date, category and unresolved identity/deposit/shelf-price status remain visible in the app.

`GET /v1/penny-berlin-publications/status` reports the latest original capture, current/quarantined cards and its refresh state. Public responses omit original bodies. The collector runs under the existing durable source lease every six hours. A 403/429 stops immediately without reading the rejected body or attempting another endpoint; native Retry-After is respected with a minimum one-hour pause. Internal eligibility ends after 24 hours or at the end of the native Berlin publication week, whichever comes first. This is a conservative eligibility limit, not an invented merchant validity interval.

Tests use clearly marked compact synthetic captures and mocked HTTP. The additional PostgreSQL-18 CI integration exercises immutable SQL storage, exact multipacks, held/removed/changed latest cards, tied captures, expiry, API validation, separate counts and isolation from canonical/receipt/other retailer tables. Test fixtures must never be imported into production.
